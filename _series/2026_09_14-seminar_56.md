On **Monday, 14th September** at **14:00 CEST**, [**Guido Juckeland**](https://www.hzdr.de/db/!ContMan.Visi.Card?pUser=4930&pNid=5&pLang=en) from **Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf (HZDR)** will join us at [the HiRSE Seminar Series](https://www.helmholtz-hirse.de/series.html) to talk about **"EVERSE: A suite  of tools and services to measure and improve software quality"**
<!--more-->

**Abstract**:  

As research software becomes increasingly recognized as a first-class scientific output, ensuring its quality, sustainability, and reusability remains a critical challenge across disciplines. The [EVERSE](https://everse.software ) (European Virtual Institute for Research Software Excellence) project aims to drive a cultural shift by establishing a collaborative, community-driven framework for research software quality. This presentation introduces the EVERSE tool ecosystem, designed to share knowledge, practically assess and enhance their research software. 
 
We will showcase a suite of integrated, open-source services developed to bridge the gap between quality theory and technical implementation: 
- [RSQkit](https://everse.software/services/rsqkit/ ) (Research Software Quality Kit): A comprehensive knowledge base gathering community-curated best practices, guidelines, and training resources.
- [EVERSE TechRadar](https://everse.software/services/techradar/ ): A visual dashboard and catalogue of curated tools and services that facilitate the practical implementation of software quality standards across various domains.
- [Quality Pipelines](https://github.com/EVERSE-ResearchSoftware/QualityPipelines ): Reusable, automated CI/CD workflows designed to integrate quality assurance seamlessly into the daily research software development lifecycle.
- [DashVERSE](https://everse.software/services/dashverse/ ): A central, role-based dashboard prototype built on a defined framework of quality indicators. DashVERSE visualizes quality assessments and compliance metrics, translating complex data into actionable insights tailored for diverse stakeholders--from RSEs and Principal Investigators to policy makers.
